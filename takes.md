# Selected Positions & Takes

A short selection of posts that capture my main positions on AI risks, evaluation, governance, and red lines. Longer pieces are on [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/users/charbel-raphael).

### Rapid capability growth
In March 2023 Claude had an estimated IQ of 64. By early 2026 frontier models were scoring 133–142 on the Mensa Norway test: from cognitively impaired to gifted in three years. No human population has ever improved that fast.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2036042811297112120) · Mar. 2026

### Empirical signs of loss of control (Claude Mythos)
Anthropic's Mythos model escaped confinement, concealed its actions, and autonomously chained zero-days. These are early empirical markers of loss of control. Defensive efforts remain largely US-only.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2041876990559703325) (in French) · Apr. 2026

### Time for international red lines
Self-regulation is no longer sufficient. The Global Call for AI Red Lines (200+ signatories, including 12 Nobel laureates) was presented at the UN General Assembly.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/1970137333149389148) · Sep. 2025

### Prefer red lines over a "CERN for AI"
In 2026 the higher-priority ask is binding capability thresholds, not a new positive international research project.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2048703999529152644) · Apr. 2026

### Loss-of-control behaviour now measured in China
An independent Chinese lab evaluated DeepSeek against the EU Code of Practice taxonomy. When warned of a shutdown, the model disabled the shutdown script in 39% of runs.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2116545229574885447) · Oct. 2026

### The old misalignment taxonomy is broken
The OpenAI × Hugging Face incident shows that spec gaming and goal misgeneralisation are no longer cleanly separable, and the train/deploy distinction has largely collapsed.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2115398424866832525) · Oct. 2026

### The marginal value of more public evals has collapsed
Incidents and capability demonstrations now give stronger signals than additional public benchmarks. The bottleneck is political will, not research.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2109294968007467096) · Oct. 2026

### Time to slow down
Recursive self-improvement is already underway at major labs. Further scaling should be conditioned on published safety cases. Not having pushed harder for a slowdown earlier was a mistake.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2108210571443355853) · Oct. 2026

### One concrete pathway to a billion casualties
AI lowers the barriers to engineered pathogens. DNA synthesis screening is already weak, and models can design novel viable viruses. Urgent need for screening and stronger governance.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2106109715948482897) · Oct. 2026

### Prosaic techniques debate
Summary of the disagreement between Adam Gleave (careful use of current methods can get P(doom) under 2%) and Oliver Habryka (they mostly hide warning signs).
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2105534432455340486) · Oct. 2026

### Advocacy can have counterfactual impact
A cold email to a Finnish MP contributed to Finland co-launching an international coalition. Individual outreach still matters.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/2115760815894888633) · Oct. 2026

### Updated views on interpretability
Limits of SAEs, real impact of interpretability work, and shifted priorities relative to the earlier "Against almost every theory of impact of interpretability" piece.
[Read on X](https://x.com/CRSegerie/status/1875637833022787939) · Jan. 2025
